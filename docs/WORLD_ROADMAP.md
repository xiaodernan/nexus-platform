## The structural canon nobody else owns

1. **The "trade-across-the-courtyard" arc** — three chapters linked by one shared supplier (say, a vegetable vendor in Yau Ma Tei): Chapter 110 「檔口的秤」→ Chapter 111 「貨van 的冬眠」→ Chapter 112 「一間收獲的廚房」 read in that order, each from a different protagonist.
2. **The "one fridge, one family" novella in six chapters** (113–118) — one Hong Kong family, six chapters over six months, ending in a rent-ceiling dispute.
3. **The "two cities at dawn" mirrored chapters** (119–124) — same hour, same scene, one in HK and one in Shenzhen; same protagonist, different city, ending with a gesture both share.
4. **The "things that are gone" arc** (125–140) — each chapter honours one thing the city stopped doing: the last cassette shop, the last phone box, the last homemade-sesame-oil maker, the last newspaper delivery boy.
